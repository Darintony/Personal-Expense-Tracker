How to Import Password-Protected Statements

Almost all bank portals encrypt downloaded statements (PDF, Excel, or CSV files) to secure your transaction ledger. Since FinTrack processes your data 100% locally on your computer inside your browser tab (never sending details to any server), it cannot decrypt these files natively. Follow this quick guide to upload successfully:

1. Common Password Formats
  • SBI Yono: Last 4 digits of your registered mobile number + @ + Date of Birth in DDMM format (e.g., 9876@2405).
  • HDFC Bank: Your Customer ID (check your passbook/netbanking).
  • ICICI Bank: First 4 characters of your name in lowercase + Date & Month of Birth in DDMM format (e.g., amit2405).
2. Remove Password Protection
  • Open the Excel sheet in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets, enter your password, and save/download a copy. Google Sheets downloads are automatically decrypted.
  • In Excel: Go to File → Info → Protect Workbook → Encrypt with Password. Clear the password string, press OK, and save.
Privacy Guarantee: Your statements are parsed entirely inside your browser cache. No financial data is ever collected or sent over the internet!
