FinTrack

Advanced Personal Expense Tracker — manage, analyze, and master your finances.

Purpose

FinTrack helps individuals take full control of their personal finances. Track every rupee, set budgets, analyze spending patterns, and generate detailed reports — all stored securely in your browser's local storage with no server required.

Features

  • 📊 Interactive Dashboard with Charts
  • 💰 Income & Expense Tracking
  • 🎯 Monthly Budget & Category Management
  • 🧠 Smart Financial Insights & Alerts
  • 📈 Advanced Statistics & Analytics
  • 📑 Daily/Weekly/Monthly/Yearly Reports
  • 🏷️ Custom Category Management
  • 🔍 Live Search & Advanced Filters
  • 📤 Import/Export (CSV, Excel, PDF)
  • 💾 Backup & Restore (JSON)
  • 🌙 Dark Mode & Theme Customization
  • 📱 Fully Responsive Design
  • 🔔 Toast Notifications
  • 🔑 Premium local Account Signup & Log In (using Username, Email, and Password)
  • 🔒 Optional device-level App Passcode PIN Lock configured from settings
  • 🏦 Smart Bank Statement Importer (SBI Yono etc. with fuzzy headers, separate Debit/Credit columns mapping, and review dashboard)
  • 🏷️ Fuzzy Auto-Categorizer (narrative auto-tagging)
  • 💵 Student Daily Survival Calculator (pocket money allocation)
  • 📊 50/30/20 Rule Budgeting Split & Advice
  • ⏰ Recurring Bill & Subscription due calendar ("Mark Paid" logic)
  • 👥 Hostel Roommate Bill Splitter (Splitwise minimal debt matrix & Log Share ledger sync)
  • 🤝 Direct Peer-to-Peer Loans (Lent / Borrowed) Ledger with one-click settlement sync
  • 🐷 Piggy Bank Savings Goals (progress trackers)
  • 🔒 Impulsive Buying Wishlist (30-day cooling-off lock)
  • 📝 Persistent Quick Notepad Drawer (autosaves)

Technologies Used
